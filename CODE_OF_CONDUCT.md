# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and unwanted sexual attention are not.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving actionable, empathetic feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility and correcting mistakes;
- keeping discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or repeated off-topic conduct
- Any behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this standard. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/emirhancaman). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
